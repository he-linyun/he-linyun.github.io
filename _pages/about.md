<div style="text-align: justify;" markdown="1">

I am a postdoctoral researcher at [HEC Montreal](https://www.hec.ca/en/), working with [Dr. Fausto Errico](https://www.gerad.ca/en/people/3417), [Dr. Jorge E. Mendoza](https://www.hec.ca/en/profs/jorge.mendoza.html), [Dr. Carolina Osorio](https://www.carolinaosorio.net) and [Dr. Tommaso Schettini](https://www.concordia.ca/faculty/tommaso-schettini.html).
I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research from the [School of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://www.isye.gatech.edu/) at Georgia Tech, where I was advised by [Dr. Eunhye Song](https://sites.google.com/view/eunhyesongphd/home).

My research interests lie across many topics.  
* Methodologies: simulation optimization, sequential decision making and experiment design, Bayesian optimization, stochastic model validation and calibration, and uncertainty quantification.
* Applications: digital twins, transportation systems, clinical trials, and smart manufacturing.
